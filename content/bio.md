I am an undergraduate researcher in engineering mechanics at **Zhejiang University**, where I work with the research group of **Prof. Jizhou Song**. My research interests center on heterogeneous interfacial detachment, stretchable electronics, transfer printing, and contact mechanics.

My current work combines experimental mechanics, analytical modeling, and finite element simulation. I have developed experiments for laser-induced droplet transfer printing and studied large-deformation contact and adhesion during a summer research appointment with **Prof. Changhong Linghu** at City University of Hong Kong.

I expect to complete my Bachelor of Engineering in June 2027 and am preparing for doctoral study in mechanics and related areas.
