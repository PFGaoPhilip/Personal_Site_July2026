I am an undergraduate researcher in engineering mechanics at **Zhejiang University**, where I work with **Prof. Jizhou Song** on laser droplet transfer printing. My research interests center on heterogeneous interfacial detachment, stretchable electronics, transfer printing, and contact mechanics.

Since September 2026, my graduation design with **Prof. L. Jay Guo** at the **University of Michigan** has focused on **Laser-Driven Perfluorocarbon Droplet Cavitation Transfer**. I developed analytical and COMSOL models connecting laser heating and perfluorocarbon phase change with cavitation, jet formation, and transmitted loads, and compared liquid and hydrogel environments to guide transfer-printing experiments. **My arrival at the University of Michigan is expected in December 2026.**

My work in Prof. Song's group combines experimental mechanics, analytical modeling, and finite element simulation. I built and optimized a laser droplet-transfer setup, measured ejection velocity and trajectory, and contributed to theoretical analysis and experimental design. Our manuscript, **Reusable Laser-Driven Droplet Stamp Transfer Printing via Laser-Induced Cavitation Jet Actuation**, is under review at *Advanced Functional Materials*.

From July to October 2026, I worked with **Prof. Changhong Linghu** at **City University of Hong Kong** on large-deformation contact of compressible hyperelastic spheres and confined adhesion in laser transfer printing. This work combined analytical corrections beyond Hertz theory, finite element modeling, specimen preparation, and compression experiments.

I expect to complete my Bachelor of Engineering in June 2027 and am preparing for doctoral study in mechanics and related areas.
